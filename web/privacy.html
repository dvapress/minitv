Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · LeapDrama (PC Web, Mobile H5 & Android App)

LeapDrama is a vertical micro-drama and comic-drama streaming platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication: Email address, verification records, or OAuth identity used to sign in and sync your favorites, watch history, Coin balance, and VIP membership across devices.
  • Playback & Engagement: Series and episodes watched, resume timestamps, likes, favorites, comments, and check-in / reward task completions.
  • Billing & Transactions: Coin top-up and membership order records. Card and wallet payments are processed securely by licensed payment providers (such as Stripe); LeapDrama never stores raw card numbers or CVV codes.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Basic browser/app version, OS type, language, theme, and anonymized playback diagnostics used to ensure smooth video delivery and prevent abuse.

2. How We Use Information

  • Deliver fast, adaptive vertical video playback and resume progress across PC web, mobile H5, and Android.
  • Process episode unlocks, Coin rewards, VIP entitlements, and customer support inquiries.
  • Protect content copyrights, enforce signed-URL playback security, and detect fraudulent or automated traffic.

4. Data Sharing, Transfers & Retention

We do not sell your personal information. We only share minimal necessary data with infrastructure, CDN, and licensed payment partners under strict confidentiality obligations, or when required by applicable law. Account data is retained while your account remains active and deleted or anonymized upon verified deletion request.

5. Minors' Privacy

LeapDrama is not directed at children under the minimum digital consent age in their jurisdiction (typically 13–16). If we learn that personal data was collected from a minor without required parental consent, we will promptly delete it.

6. Your Rights & Contact

Depending on your region (including GDPR, UK GDPR, POPIA, NDPR, or CCPA), you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data at any time via account settings or by emailing privacy@leapdrama.app.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 · LeapDrama（PC 网页端、移动端 H5 及 Android 客户端）

LeapDrama 是一款面向全球用户的竖屏微短剧与漫剧发行平台。本隐私政策详细说明我们在您使用服务时如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息，以及您享有的隐私权利。

一、我们收集的信息

  • 账号与登录信息：用于注册、验证码登录或第三方授权登录的邮箱及账号标识，以便在不同设备间同步您的收藏、观影进度、金币余额与会员状态。
  • 观影与互动记录：您观看的剧集与集数、断点续播位置、点赞、收藏、评论及每日签到/任务奖励记录。
  • 交易与账单信息：金币充值、剧集解锁与会员订单流水。银行卡或钱包支付由海外持牌支付机构（如 Stripe）安全处理，LeapDrama 不采集也不存储您的银行卡明文信息或安全码。
  • 设备与诊断日志：基础浏览器/客户端版本、操作系统类型、语言与深浅色偏好，以及匿名化播放性能指标，用于保障高清流畅播放与防刷风控。

二、信息使用目的

  • 为您提供稳定的竖屏视频串流服务，并在 PC 网页、移动端 H5 与 Android 客户端间同步观影记录。
  • 完成剧集解锁、金币发放、会员权益开通以及客服工单核实与退款处理。
  • 保障内容版权安全、校验签名播放链路，防范恶意抓包、盗链及异常流量攻击。

四、信息共享、跨境传输与保留期限

我们绝不出售您的个人数据，仅在提供云基础设施、CDN 加速与持牌支付结算所必需的最小范围内，向受严格保密协议约束的合作服务商共享信息，或依法律法规强制要求提供。您的账号数据在账号存续期间保留，注销或申请删除后将依法及时删除或匿名化处理。

五、未成年人保护

如您未达到所在地区规定的数字服务最低同意年龄（通常为 13–16 周岁），请在监护人同意与陪同下使用本服务。如发现未经监护人同意收集了未成年人信息，我们将尽快予以删除。

六、您的权利与联系我们

您可随时在「个人中心」管理偏好设置，或发送邮件至 privacy@leapdrama.app 申请查阅、更正、导出或删除您的个人账号数据，我们将在合理法定期限内响应。
